A Letter From 10-Amethyst

Dear Maam Fabello,

As the whole 10-Amethyst section, we would like to take this opportunity to say thank you for everything you have done for us.

We know that being a teacher is not always easy. There are times when our class can be noisy, our work can be incomplete, or we don't understand a lesson right away. But despite all of that, you continue to teach us, guide us, and give us chances to improve.

Thank you for every lesson, activity, reminder, and every time you patiently explained something we didn't understand. These simple moments have become memories that we will remember as a class.

Through TLE and programming, you have taught us more than just how to code or create webpages. You have also taught us to be creative, patient, responsible, and willing to keep trying when things don't go as planned.

We may not always be the perfect students, but we hope you know that we truly appreciate your effort, patience, and dedication.

From every student in 10-Amethyst , thank you for believing in us, guiding us, and being part of our journey.

Happy Teachers' Day! 💚📚

With appreciation and gratitude,
Your students, 10-Amethyst 💚
